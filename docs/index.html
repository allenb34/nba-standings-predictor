How the predictions are doing

Pace wins = current win% × 82. MAE = mean absolute gap between predicted and pace wins, across all 30 teams. Spearman ρ compares the predicted order with the actual order inside each conference (1 = identical, 0 = unrelated). Early in the season pace wins swing a lot, so treat these numbers as noisy until roughly 20 games in.

Revised before tip-off. The official predictions are v1.1, re-locked on Oct 19 after a review found the first version (v1) undervalued stars returning from injury, such as Tyrese Haliburton. The change was made after seeing v1's numbers, and its validation gain is not statistically reliable, so the original v1 predictions are shown and scored alongside. Read the full disclosure.

Reading the very top projections. In past-season testing, the model's projections above 60 wins came in about 3.5 wins too high on average, and 6 of 7 such teams finished below their projection. Below 60 wins, it has been unbiased: on average, teams finished within about half a win of their projection, though an individual team typically misses by about 7 wins. So Oklahoma City's 69.7 is best read as a mid-60s team with upside. This note was added after the lock; the locked predictions are unchanged.
